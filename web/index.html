Wet vent conditions (7.5.2.1) — the size is only half the answer

At most 2 water closets, connected at the same level, and downstream of every other fixture. Trap arms and fixture drains into the wet vent are limited to NPS 2 (emergency floor drain excepted). On multi-storey wet vents, any storey above the first may drain at most 4 FU. Only one horizontal offset is permitted (≤ 1.2 m for NPS 2 and smaller, ≤ 2.5 m above NPS 2), and the wet-vented portion may not reduce in size. Length, however, is not limited.

Circuit venting (7.5.3.1) in one paragraph

All circuit-vented fixtures must be on one floor, with no stack upstream. A relief vent connects downstream of the last fixture (a drain carrying ≤ 6 FU may double as it). Add an additional circuit vent when the branch turns more than 45° in total between vent connections or serves more than 8 fixtures between them. Minimum branch size: NPS 2 (traps under NPS 2) or NPS 3 (traps NPS 2 and up). Circuit vent size itself comes from Table 7.5.8.3 — not reproduced here.
